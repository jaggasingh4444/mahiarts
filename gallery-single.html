Handmade Picture Study

Maheswori's handmade pictures are created with careful observation, simple materials, and a focus on personal meaning. A custom piece can begin from a reference photo, a memory, a favorite place, a plant, or a gift idea.

For commissions, share the subject, preferred size, color preference, deadline, and any reference images. The final direction can be adjusted for portraits, decorative art, nature studies, or keepsake gifts.

Her plant science background gives her an attentive eye for organic lines, leaf patterns, and field textures, which makes nature-inspired pieces especially personal.

Pricing and timing depend on complexity and size, so the best next step is to send the request details directly.
